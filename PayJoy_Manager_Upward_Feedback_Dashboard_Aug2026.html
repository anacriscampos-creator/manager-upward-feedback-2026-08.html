PayJoy//Manager Upward Feedback Dashboard — August 2026
CONFIDENTIAL · HR USE ONLY

August 2026 Manager Upward Feedback. During the August 2026 review cycle, 287 managers received upward feedback from their teams. Overall manager effectiveness is strong at 4.54 / 5, with Development Conversations the lowest-rated dimension (4.38 / 5) and the clearest company-wide opportunity. 58 managers (20%) fell below the PayJoy average across all four categories.

What the data shows. The strongest signal is tenure: 40% of first-year managers fall below on all four, and the 58 are far newer than average (median 1.5 vs 2.9 years) — pointing at onboarding and first-time-manager support rather than seniority, since below-rates are flat across levels (13–25%, no trend). Struggling concentrates in Peru (47% of its managers) and, by function, in Operations and Finance; Product Development is healthiest. The shortfall is broad rather than one failing area — these managers score below the company average on all four categories, not just one. 42 of the 58 have 3+ responses and are the reliable core to act on first.
